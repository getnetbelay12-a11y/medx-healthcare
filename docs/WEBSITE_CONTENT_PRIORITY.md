# MedX Website Content Priority

This checklist separates launch-critical content from material that can be added later.

## High Priority: Required Before Public Launch

- Final domain name and preferred canonical website URL
- Official MedX logo in high resolution
- Exact office address in Ethiopia / Amhara
- Local phone number
- International phone number or WhatsApp number
- Official email addresses and contact routing
- Final approved company profile
- Confirmed leadership list, titles, short bios, and approved photos
- Approval for what can be published about AVC, PAHO/WHO, OncoE6, and other partners
- Approved real photos of office, team, training, facility, and dignitary visits
- Written consent for any patient photo, story, or clinical case
- Final legal/privacy/contact-form approval
- Final launch approver

## Medium Priority: Important During Development

- Mission, vision, and values
- Detailed service descriptions
- Product and product-category descriptions
- Brochures or catalogs approved for public use
- Public-health program descriptions
- OncoE6 program summary approved by the MedX and AVC teams
- Partner names and logos with explicit permission
- Social media links
- Google Maps location
- Office, laboratory, product, and training photos

## Lower Priority: Can Be Added After Launch

- News and announcements
- Events and conference participation
- Training updates
- Community outreach stories
- Success stories
- Videos
- Publications and resources
- Awards and certificates
- Future project updates
- Expanded photo gallery

## Attachment Use

The attached OncoE6, HPV, PAHO, ICO/IARC, and Ethiopia guideline materials are useful for public-health credibility. They should support short summaries, sourced statistics, and program language.

Do not copy journal articles or manuals directly into the website. Do not publish confidential slides, patient images, partner logos, or clinical images without written approval.
